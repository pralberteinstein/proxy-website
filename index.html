rules.md

What your EA knows · learned in week one

  1. Look at the calendar first. They may already be in a room together.
  2. Offer two options. Say which one is better for us.
  3. Always give a fallback, so a no doesn't end the thread.
  4. Be honest about the cost to the other side. “That's 5 AM for them.”
  5. Second change? Apologise once. Explain briefly.
  6. Don't move meetings on a Friday.
  7. Batch yes/no questions so the founder answers in one reply.
  8. When they change something themselves, ask why. Keep the rule.
  9. Tell them about a mistake before they find it.
