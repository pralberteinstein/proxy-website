02Recent work

Client testimonial

“You always take care of things and it doesn't get dropped.”

— Our first client, Sep 15
  1. 09-10

    A founder asked the EA to set up an in-person interview day for a job candidate. Over the next two days it grew: a second candidate, an NDA to confirm, both interviews running later than planned, a side meeting for the founder, and three meeting rooms at a shared office the EA had no login for. The EA found out who on the founder's team had one, got the rooms booked, and sent every invite with its own agenda, while emailing and Slacking everyone in real time to keep them in the loop.

  2. 09-14

    The founder needed an urgent one-hour meeting with four colleagues the next day, before one of them left for Europe. The founder's afternoon was already full. The EA noticed all five were already in a 6:30 PM meeting together and asked the person who ran it to start 30 minutes earlier, so the urgent meeting could follow straight after. He didn't reply for 45 minutes. The EA followed up once and offered to leave his meeting as it was if moving it was a hassle. He moved it, and all five met at 7 PM.

  3. 09-15

    A job candidate was 14 hours ahead, so most times that suited the founder fell in the middle of the candidate's night. The EA's first plan, an evening call for the founder, fell through when the founder's evenings filled up. The recruiter turned down the next option, 5 AM for the candidate, and asked for 9 AM the founder's time instead. The founder had a meeting at 9, so the EA asked if they could start at 8:30. They agreed, and the interview was booked.

  4. 09-16

    The client decided at the last minute to send notebooks to a second conference. The vendor they'd used before couldn't make them in time, so the EA found a new one and tracked the order against the deadline. The shipments to Florida and Canada went out, but the Canadian one was held at customs and the vendor couldn't clear it. The EA found a third vendor that ships within Canada. That vendor then ran out of stock. The EA is still working on it.
